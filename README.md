# Content Marketing Campaign & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Project Title
**Content Marketing Campaign & Customer Analytics Dashboard | Power BI**

## 2. Short Description / Purpose
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze marketing campaign performance, customer purchasing behavior, lead sources, conversions, and return on investment (ROI).

## 3. Tech Stack
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures
- **Excel / CSV** – Data storage and analysis

## 4. Data Source
Dataset sourced from **Kaggle**, containing customer information, marketing campaign performance, and purchase-related data.

## 5. Features / Highlights

### a) Business Problem
Businesses need to understand which marketing sources generate the most purchases, how effectively campaigns convert customers, and where marketing budgets deliver the best returns.

### b) Key Features
- KPI cards for Total Conversions, CPA, and ROI
- Lead generation analysis by purchase source
- ROI comparison across different years
- Purchase distribution by store, web, and catalog channels
- Interactive visuals for quick performance analysis

### c) Business Impact & Insights
- Identifies the strongest purchase channels.
- Highlights differences in campaign performance across years.
- Helps evaluate customer purchasing behavior.
- Supports data-driven marketing budget allocation and campaign optimization.

### d) Project Goal
To transform raw marketing and customer data into actionable insights that help businesses improve campaign effectiveness, optimize spending, and make better marketing decisions.
